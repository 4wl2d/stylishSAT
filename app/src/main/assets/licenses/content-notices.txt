StylishSAT original draft content · full bank

Lessons, passages, tasks, fictional chart values, listening scripts and feedback samples were authored for this project using AI assistance. They have machine validation but no independent human expert review. They are not official SAT or IELTS materials and do not supply calibrated SAT scores or IELTS bands.

Synthetic Listening and Speaking audio (content package 7 and later):
All 24 Listening recordings and 6 Speaking samples were re-recorded from the unchanged original scripts with Piper neural text-to-speech (piper-tts 1.8.0, GPL-3.0-or-later, used only during authoring and not bundled). Conversations use a different voice for each speaker; recordings mix British and American English voices: Cori (en_GB), John, Norman, Kristin and LJ Speech (en_US), from the rhasspy/piper-voices catalog (MIT repository licence, revision c10ece1aade47bb51c153c893d14e5bf8e5b7117). Their model cards list public-domain training recordings (LibriVox; the LJ Speech dataset). No voice model is bundled. Machine transcription checks passed; human listening review is pending.
https://github.com/OHF-voice/piper1-gpl
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices

Earlier eSpeak NG audio (content packages up to 6, kept so earlier answers reopen with the audio they used):
Generated from original scripts using eSpeak NG 1.52.0 formant synthesis. Legacy Listening clips use en-us at155words/minute. Expanded Listening clips use en-us/en-gb at150or170words/minute; Speaking examples use en-us/en-gb/en-gb-scotland at155–165words/minute. Listening recordings announce their synthetic origin; Speaking examples are labeled synthetic in the player. No Apple System Voices, cloned human voices, official recordings or speech-synthesis executable are bundled. The open-source eSpeak NG engine was used only during authoring. New clips are delivered as Ogg/Opus; Reproducibility manifests are included in the public source; duplicate WAV masters remain in the local authoring workspace.
Engine source and GPL-3.0-or-later licence: https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng/tree/1.52.0
